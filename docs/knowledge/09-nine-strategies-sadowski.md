# 9 Advanced and Profitable Trading Strategies — Roman Sadowski (HumbleTraders)

Source text: 42 pages (pdftotext), read in full. Market: written for **forex** (USD crosses, EURUSD, GBPJPY, USDJPY), with passing mention of stocks and commodities. Short marketing e-book; many charts are missing from the text (only captions survive). Units are forex "points/pips".

## Main ideas (p. 2-4, p. 40-42)
- Successful day traders do three things: use tried-and-tested strategies, execute them with 100% discipline, keep strict money management (p. 2).
- Few people make a living day trading; mostly those using other people's money (p. 3).
- A rule-based method turns trading into a "binary decision rather than an emotional decision" and prevents over-trading (p. 41).
- Three causes of failure: no/untested strategy (trading on hearsay/TV), lack of discipline, bad or absent money management (p. 41).
- Table of contents (p. 2) also lists "RSI Trading Strategy, 5 Systems + Back Test Results" and a "Binary options ... 150% return" strategy; **neither appears in the body text**. The body contains 9 numbered sections (one section, candlesticks, has four patterns).

## 1. Momentum Reversal strategy (p. 5-7)
- Combines fundamentals (Commitments of Traders report to set mid/long-term bias) with technicals.
- Tools: multi-timeframe Stochastic, support/resistance zones, Fibonacci retracements (no settings given).
- Daily-chart checklist: (1) has the market clearly fallen/rallied recently? (2) are weekly AND daily stochastics overbought/oversold (above 70 / below 30)? (3) is price at a major S/R zone? (p. 6)
- If yes, mark the zone and drop to intraday charts to look for a reversal price pattern (pattern not specified).
- Trades placed as pending limit orders, often filled in London session; 1-5 signals/month on USD majors; holds up to several weeks (p. 5).
- Trade only in the fundamental trend direction; counter-trend setups either skipped or taken with smaller size (p. 7).

## 2. Moving average crossover (p. 8-11)
- Three MAs: 20 (fast), 60 (slow), 100 (trend). MA type not stated.
- Buy when 20 crosses above 60; sell when it crosses below; close the position when they cross back (p. 8).
- If bars stay consistently on one side of the 100 MA a strong trend is in force — let the trade run.
- Shorter settings give more false signals (p. 8). False signals cluster in ranging markets (p. 9).
- Example EURUSD daily, 6 months: four winners (600, 200, 200, 100 pts), one breakeven, one -35; "correct 6/12 or 50% of the time" (p. 9-11).

## 3. Heikin-Ashi reversal (p. 11-21)
- HA candle: close = avg(O,H,L,C); open = avg(prev HA open, prev HA close); high/low = extreme of high/open/close (p. 11). Smoother, lagging, no repaint after close (p. 12).
- Daily charts only; pattern valid when two reversal candles are fully completed.
- Long setup: two consecutive green HA candles after a series of red ones (p. 14). Short is the mirror.
- Momentum filter: Stochastic (14,7,3). p. 15 text says "enter long after two consecutive RED candles ... Stochastic above 70" — this contradicts p. 14 and looks like a typo/inversion; the consistent reading is long after two GREEN candles with Stochastic coming from oversold (below 30).
- Stop-order filter: buy-stop a few pips above the high of the second reversal candle (p. 15).
- Optional Accelerator Oscillator filter: for longs, wait for a green AC bar above 0 on daily (p. 16).
- Avoid entering right after a violent one-way swing; avoid bank holidays, NFP, FOMC, central-banker speeches (p. 17).
- Money management (p. 18): move to break-even after +50 pips; trail stop to major local lows/highs or exit on opposite signal; stop 100 pips flat or at local technical levels; let winners run.
- Few setups, but "usually important market tops or bottoms". Three sample trades: +300, +130, +170 pips (p. 19-21).

## 4. Swing (correction) strategy (p. 22-25)
- Corrections = lots of overlapping bars and contracting range; trends move fast, corrections do not (p. 22).
- Example EURUSD 4h: 26 candles within 100 pts, daily range shrinking to 20 pts — "HIGH ALERT" (p. 23).
- Trade in direction of the prior trend: enter when price breaks out of the contracting range of the last few candles; stop at the most recent minor swing (p. 23).
- Long example: after a downside correction, two candles closed above the range top; buy the open of the next candle, stop at the lowest point of the correction. Risk ~30 pts vs ~600 pts gain (p. 24-25).

## 5. Candlestick patterns (p. 25-29)
- Bullish engulfing: real body covers the body of one or more previous candles; more candles engulfed = stronger. Wait for the following candle then enter; stop at engulfing candle low (p. 26). Bearish is the mirror (p. 27).
- Long shadow: shadow at least 2x the real body; appears at turning points. Enter at open of next candle; stop at the shadow extreme, trailed with the trend (p. 28).
- Hammer: body at one end of the candle ("head and handle"). Enter next open; stop at the extreme; trail (p. 29).

## 6. Role Reversal (p. 29-34)
- S/R are zones, not exact prices (p. 30). Broken resistance becomes support in uptrends (p. 32-33).
- Uptrend = higher highs and higher lows; only longs; "buy on pullbacks" (p. 31).
- Pullbacks may go to the old resistance, a Fibonacci level, a pivot point, or a moving average (p. 33-34).
- Scaled entry: split position into thirds with limit orders — 1/3 at 20 MA, 1/3 at the role-reversal level, 1/3 at 50% Fib (p. 34). Aggressive days only reach the 20 MA; others dip to 38% Fib.

## 7. Bollinger Band squeeze (p. 35-36)
- BB(20, 2) with Band Width indicator on EURUSD 4h. Look for contraction with band width approaching 0.0100 (~100 pts) (p. 36).
- Buy when a full candle completes above the middle (SMA) line; sell when below.
- Stop at the low (high for shorts) of the preceding candle, or at a 3% capital loss, whichever is smaller (p. 36).

## 8. Narrow Range (p. 36-38)
- Very short-term. Find the narrowest-range candle of the past 4 or 7 days (NR4/NR7). Consecutive narrow candles foreshadow bigger breakouts (p. 37).
- Buy when price moves above the narrow candle's high; sell below its low. Stop at the opposite end of the narrow candle, trailed (p. 38).

## 9. 2-period RSI (p. 39-40)
- Buy when RSI(2) moves above 90; sell when it moves below 10 (a momentum, not mean-reversion, use of RSI(2)).
- Very aggressive; whipsaws in ranges with several losers in a row; needs rigorous stops (p. 39-40).
- In trends, add positions on further BUY triggers; close all on the opposing signal (p. 40).

## Risk & money management
- "3 / 1000 rule": never risk more than 3% of capital per trade; never risk more than 1/1000 of capital per point (p. 42).
- Always use a stop loss (p. 42). HA strategy: break-even at +50 pips, 100-pip flat stop (p. 18). BB: stop min(prior candle extreme, 3% loss) (p. 36).
- Counter-trend trades with reduced size (p. 7). Scaling into thirds lowers risk (p. 34).

## Psychology
- Trading is hard work, not a pastime; claimed 93% fail, ~4.5% succeed (p. 40).
- Do not trade on tips/TV narratives; anecdote of a $1000 account wiped by a 200-pt move at $5/pt (p. 41).
- Rules remove emotional rationalisation and over-trading (p. 11, 41).

## What the author says doesn't work
- Shorter MA settings: more false signals (p. 8). MA crossovers fail in ranges (p. 9).
- Raw HA candle flip without filters is not enough (p. 14).
- RSI(2) gets chopped up in ranging markets (p. 39).
- Trading without a stop or on hearsay (p. 41-42).

## Claims to treat with caution
- "can cut your losses by 63% immediately" from learning basic strategies (p. 4) — no evidence.
- Momentum Reversal "traded in live markets for the last 15 months", performance "clearly documented" — not included (p. 5).
- HA strategy "proven to be very powerful in back test and live trading" (p. 13); only 3 cherry-picked winning examples shown.
- MA crossover winners of 600/200/200/100 points from a hand-picked 6-month chart (p. 9).
- Swing example ~30 pt risk for ~600 pt gain (p. 25) — single hindsight example.
- "93% lose", "4.5% succeed" statistics (p. 40) unsourced.
